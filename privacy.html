Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you donate or contact us, such as your name, email and donation details, plus basic analytics about how you use our site.

2. How We Use It

To process donations, send receipts and impact updates, respond to enquiries, and improve our services. We never sell your data.

3. Payment Security

Payments are processed by PCI-compliant providers. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

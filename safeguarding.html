Safeguarding Our Communities

AFF has implemented child safeguarding initiatives through school and community platforms to promote safer environments and strengthen the protection and wellbeing of children and adolescents.

Our Safeguarding Commitment

Safeguarding is a core organizational value. We are uncompromising in our commitment to protecting the rights, dignity, and safety of all people—particularly children, women, and vulnerable populations—across all our operations globally.

This commitment is embedded in our policies, culture, systems, and accountability structures. Every team member, partner, and stakeholder shares responsibility for safeguarding.

Core Safeguarding Principles

👁️

Transparency

We operate with complete transparency in all safeguarding matters. Clear policies, open reporting channels, and public accountability standards define our approach.

🛡️

Protection

We prioritize the safety of vulnerable populations above all else. Robust protocols, prevention measures, and victim support systems are in place.

⚖️

Accountability

We hold ourselves and all partners accountable to the highest standards. Violations are investigated thoroughly and addressed decisively.

🤝

Partnership

We work with communities and local partners who share our safeguarding values. Collective responsibility drives our collective impact.

💬

Voice & Agency

We amplify the voices of those we serve. Communities lead safeguarding conversations and decisions affecting them.

🔄

Continuous Learning

We continuously train staff, update policies, and learn from incidents. Safeguarding excellence requires ongoing commitment.

Our Safeguarding Framework

Child Protection Policy

Comprehensive policies protecting children from abuse, exploitation, and neglect. All staff undergo vetting, training, and annual certification. Programs are designed with child protection at the center.

Key components: Screening processes • Code of conduct • Incident reporting • Support services • Community engagement

Prevention of Sexual Abuse & Harassment (PSEA)

Zero tolerance for sexual abuse, harassment, or exploitation. Prevention training for all staff. Safe reporting mechanisms for survivors. Victim-centered support and accountability measures.

Key components: Prevention training • Confidential reporting • Investigation protocols • Survivor support • Disciplinary action

Code of Conduct

Clear behavioral standards for all staff, volunteers, and partners. Covers professional conduct, conflict of interest, data protection, and ethical decision-making. Violations subject to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.

Key components: Professional conduct • Ethical behavior • Reporting obligations • Conflict of interest • Disciplinary procedures

Accessibility & Accountability

Multiple confidential reporting channels for safeguarding concerns. Independent investigation and oversight. Transparent accountability processes. Protection for whistleblowers. Regular audits and assessments.

Key components: Hotline • Email reporting • Anonymous channels • Investigation standards • Whistleblower protection

Victim-Centered Support

Comprehensive support for survivors of abuse or exploitation. Trauma-informed approach. Psychosocial support, legal assistance, and community resources. Confidentiality and dignity maintained throughout.

Key components: Immediate support • Counseling services • Legal aid • Community resources • Long-term follow-up

Reporting Safeguarding Concerns

How to Report

If you have concerns about safeguarding, abuse, or exploitation, please report immediately through any of the following channels:

  • Email (Confidential)
    safeguarding@ascend-futures.org
  • Phone Hotline
    +1-855-SAFE-AF (1-855-723-3236)
    Available 24/7, all languages
  • In-Person
    Contact our safeguarding office in any country where we operate
  • Anonymous Reporting
    Use our anonymous online form on this website

What We Guarantee

  • ✓ Confidentiality

    Your identity and report details remain confidential unless you wish to be identified.

  • ✓ No Retaliation

    We protect reporters from retaliation. Any adverse action is prohibited by policy.

  • ✓ Investigation

    All reports are investigated promptly and thoroughly by trained personnel.

  • ✓ Accountability

    Substantiated concerns result in disciplinary action and corrective measures.

Training & Continuous Improvement

Universal Training

All staff, volunteers, and board members receive mandatory safeguarding training upon onboarding and annually thereafter. Training is specific to role and context, covering policies, reporting procedures, and trauma-informed approaches.

Specialist Capacity

Dedicated safeguarding officers in every region. Specialized training for field teams, program managers, and leadership on risk assessment and incident response.

Independent Audits

Annual third-party audits of our safeguarding framework. Results are published publicly. We act on recommendations immediately.

Questions About Our Safeguarding Practices?

We're committed to transparency and accountability. Contact our safeguarding team or review our full policy document.

Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know before giving. Still stuck? Our team is one message away.

Founded in 2017 by two HIV prevention advocates from Zambia and Malawi and formally registered in 2024, we're a Zambian youth-focused, woman-led non-profit working on SRHR & HIV prevention, community advocacy and accountability, climate-health, and youth mental wellbeing — through youth empowerment, leadership development and sustainable community transformation — through advocacy, capacity building, research, mentorship and community engagement.
We're based in Lusaka and currently implement programmes in 12 districts across 7 provinces of Zambia — partnering with 25 health facilities, local organisations, and government.
We welcome volunteers for events, outreach and skills-based support. Head to our volunteer sign-up and we'll match you with opportunities that fit your skills and interests.
We work with companies and institutions on sponsorships, cause campaigns and matched programs. See Corporate Partnerships or email partnerships@ascendfuturesfoundation.org.
We publish our work and outcomes openly and share governance and financial information on our Financials and Impact pages.
Email info@ascendfuturesfoundation.org or use our contact form — we'd love to hear from you.

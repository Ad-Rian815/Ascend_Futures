A National Milestone

World AIDS Day 2025 & the Lenacapavir launch

Ascend Futures Foundation played a visible and catalytic role in the 2025 World AIDS Day commemoration, which also served as Zambia's official launch of long-acting injectable Lenacapavir — the Foundation's first participation in a national World AIDS Day event.

Leading up to the launch, AFF contributed community mobilisation, media engagement, training of healthcare providers and mobilisers, and the development and adaptation of information, education, and communication materials to support the Lenacapavir rollout.

The event was officiated by the Honourable Minister of Health and attended by government, partners, civil society, youth, and community members — aligned with the global theme "Overcoming Disruption, Transforming the AIDS Response." AFF acknowledges the support of AVAC, whose investment in community-centred prevention, evidence generation, and innovation was instrumental to the successful launch.

MEAL

Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning

A robust MEAL framework strengthens programme quality, tracks progress, measures impact, and promotes evidence-informed decision-making.

Monitoring

Routine programme monitoring, performance tracking against indicators, digital data collection, and quarterly & annual reviews.

Evaluation

Baseline, mid-term and end-line evaluations, impact assessments, operational research, and programme reviews.

Accountability

Community feedback and complaints response, stakeholder platforms, transparent reporting, and safeguarding & ethical compliance.

Learning

Documenting lessons and best practices, knowledge-sharing forums, adaptive management, and evidence products & policy briefs.

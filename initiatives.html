Our Programmes & Initiatives

Active projects across SRHR & HIV prevention, climate change, and child protection — implemented with communities and partners across Zambia.

SRHR & HIV Prevention

SHIELD Project

Supported by ViiV Healthcare

Strengthening Zambia's health system so that people using long-acting injectable PrEP (CAB) continue receiving services without interruption. SHIELD responds to service disruptions caused by funding changes and wellness centre closures — addressing gaps in human resources, data systems, commodity management, and client follow-up, while supporting retention, continuity of care, safe transitions between prevention options, and informed choice.

Active in 23 health facilities across 10 districts

SRHR & HIV Prevention

Her Choice, Her Power

Supported by Aidsfonds

Accelerating access to and uptake of HIV prevention options for young women and girls in East and Southern Africa. The programme equips young ambassadors with knowledge and practical skills to challenge stigma, promote accurate HIV prevention information, facilitate peer-to-peer learning, and encourage informed decision-making — building a network of young champions who influence healthier behaviours.

Implemented in Lusaka, Chongwe & Chilanga districts · active in 3 health facilities

SRHR & HIV Prevention

PrEP Ring Community Engagement

With the International Partnership for Microbicides (IPM), an affiliate of Population Council South Africa

Supporting community engagement around the Dapivirine Vaginal Ring (PrEP Ring) in Zambia — working directly with adolescent girls and young women through dialogue sessions, youth-friendly consultations, and community discussions to understand user experiences, address myths, identify barriers to access, and improve service delivery.

Young people at the centre of shaping HIV prevention

SRHR & HIV Prevention

The APHA Project — AWPCAB Academy

With Advocates for the Prevention of HIV in Africa (APHA)

A 10-month HIV prevention leadership initiative recruiting and training 20 Community Advocates to lead community education, mobilisation, and advocacy — strengthening community-led prevention and accountability across Zambia.

240+ community sessions · 12 facilities · 14,000 women to reach

Climate Change

Kapilyomba Tree Planting & Climate Resilience

Community-led initiative

Strengthening environmental conservation and community resilience to climate change. The initiative engages community members — particularly young people — in tree planting, environmental awareness, and sustainable practices that restore degraded landscapes and improve local ecosystems, fostering long-term environmental stewardship.

Community action + climate education

Child Protection

Empowering Communities to End Child Marriage

Supported through the African Union & Restless Development Uganda

Working in Chongwe District to prevent child marriage through community mobilisation, school-based interventions, and multi-stakeholder collaboration — establishing Ending Child Marriage School Clubs, strengthening partnerships with traditional leaders, schools, and government, and creating community structures for prevention, early identification, and response.

Promoting girls' education & safe environments

Initiatives for 2026–2028

Concrete programmes under each strategic goal — with targets from our implementation framework.

SRHR, HIV Prevention & Youth-Friendly Services

  • Outreach programmes in rural & underserved areas — 10,000 people across 15 communities
  • Train 200 healthcare workers on inclusive care
  • 50 health education campaigns in schools & community centres
  • Distribute 5,000 resource kits (condoms, contraceptives, IEC materials)
  • Support 5 youth-friendly health spaces with facilities & local partners

Community Systems, Accountability & Citizen-Led Advocacy

  • 10 workshops on gender equality & menstrual health
  • Train 50 community leaders to respond to gender-based violence
  • 3 policy briefs on ending child marriage & gender representation
  • 150 mentorships connecting youth with role models
  • 150 youth through leadership & entrepreneurship training
  • 150 youth-led community projects in health & accountability

Climate Resilience & Healthy Communities

  • 4 institutional learning & technical exchange activities
  • Develop & adopt 1 climate-health integration framework
  • 2 evidence products on climate-related health risks
  • 3 cross-sector partnerships for climate-sensitive health programming
  • Climate-health messaging integrated into outreach in 15 communities

Youth Mental Health & Psychosocial Wellbeing

  • Psychosocial support integrated into all community outreach
  • 20 awareness & stigma-reduction sessions with young people
  • Train 60 peer educators & volunteers in basic psychosocial support
  • 2 partnerships with specialised mental health institutions
  • Pilot & evaluate 1 youth resilience intervention for future scale-up

Research, Innovation, Learning & Evidence

  • 5 studies published on emerging health & social challenges
  • 10 policy dialogues advocating data-driven reforms
  • A functional outcomes database guiding future interventions
  • 20 staff and 70 young people & partners trained in advocacy

Targets, not trophies

These are commitments from our 2026–2028 implementation framework — indicative targets to be confirmed through annual costing and as partnerships and funding are secured.

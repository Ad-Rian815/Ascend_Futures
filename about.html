Ascend Futures Foundation (AFF) is a Zambian youth-focused and woman-led non-profit advancing equitable health, gender justice, and youth leadership.

Who We Are

Young people are best placed to lead change

Ascend Futures Foundation (AFF) is a Zambian youth-focused and woman-led non-profit organisation working to advance healthy, informed, and resilient communities. Founded in 2017 by two HIV prevention advocates from Zambia and Malawi, and formally registered as a non-profit in 2024, AFF grew out of a clear conviction: young people are not simply beneficiaries of development — they are among the people best placed to lead it.

We are based in Lusaka and work across high-burden and underserved districts in Zambia, with a focus on adolescents, young people and women who are too often left out of decisions about their own health and futures.

Our work sits at the intersection of sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR) and HIV prevention, community advocacy and systems accountability, and the rising demands of climate, health, and youth mental wellbeing. We combine community mobilisation, advocacy, research, and partnership to strengthen both community systems and the health systems meant to serve them.

🎯

Our Vision

A society where young people thrive in healthy, informed, resilient, and just communities.

🧭

Our Mission

To advance healthy, informed, resilient, and empowered communities through youth-centered, evidence-driven, and community-led solutions.

🤝

A Word From Our Executive Director

"We see young people not as recipients of development but as the people already driving it. Our role is to back them with the tools, networks, and opportunities to do that well."

Core Values

What We Stand For

🌱

Youth-Centered Leadership

Young people are powerful agents of change and must be meaningfully engaged in shaping the solutions that affect their lives and communities.

⚖️

Equity & Inclusion

We promote fairness, dignity, equal opportunity, and inclusive access to health services, information, and development opportunities for all.

🫱🏾‍🫲🏽

Community Empowerment

We value community ownership, participation, and locally driven solutions that strengthen resilience, accountability, and sustainable impact.

🛡️

Integrity & Accountability

We uphold transparency, ethical conduct, professionalism, and responsible stewardship across all our work and partnerships.

💡

Innovation & Learning

We embrace evidence-based programming, continuous learning, digital innovation, and adaptive approaches that improve impact and sustainability.

Populations We Serve

Centered on those too often left out

✔ Adolescent girls and young women (AGYW)

✔ Adolescents and young people, in and out of school

✔ Women and girls

✔ Key populations and other underserved groups

✔ Healthcare workers and peer educators

✔ Rural and peri-urban communities affected by poverty, stigma, and gender inequality

Alignment

Anchored in national & global priorities

AFF is aligned with Zambia's national development priorities, including the National HIV Prevention Roadmap 2025–2030 and the National Health Strategic Plan, as well as global commitments on HIV prevention, gender equality, and youth development.

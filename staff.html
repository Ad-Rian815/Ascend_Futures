The Team Behind the Mission

AFF is powered by a lean, committed team — supported by volunteers, interns, and community ambassadors who drive grassroots mobilisation.

Organisational Structure

How we're organised

The Executive Director leads organisational and operational management, supported by finance and administration, technical advisory, programmes, monitoring & evaluation, communications and advocacy, and IT functions.

🧑🏾‍💼

Executive Director

Leads organisational leadership and operational management, supported by a Technical Advisor across thematic areas.

🤝

Business Development & Partnerships

Resource mobilisation, partnerships, proposal coordination, and donor relations.

🧾

Finance & Administration

Financial management, administration, human resources, IT, and operational systems — led by the Finance & Administration Director.

🌱

Programmes

Programme design, implementation, technical quality, and learning — including Program Officers and the Communications & Advocacy Officer.

📊

Monitoring & Evaluation

MEAL systems, research, and evidence generation — including M&E Officers and Researchers.

📣

Community Ambassadors — "Ascenders"

Trusted young people recruited from within the communities they serve, supporting grassroots mobilisation and community engagement — alongside interns and volunteers.

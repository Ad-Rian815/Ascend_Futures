The Team Behind the Mission

AFF is powered by a lean, committed team — supported by volunteers, interns, and community ambassadors who drive grassroots mobilisation.

Organisational Structure

How we're organised

The Executive Director leads organisational and operational management, supported by finance and administration, technical advisory, programmes, monitoring & evaluation, communications and advocacy, and IT functions.

Executive Director

Leads organisational leadership and operational management, supported by Technical Advisors across thematic areas.

Technical Advisors

Technical Advisors provide specialised expertise and strategic guidance to strengthen the quality, relevance, evidence base, and impact of AFF’s programmes. They support programme design, technical standards, capacity strengthening, research and learning, monitoring and evaluation, and policy advocacy across our priority areas.

Business Development & Partnerships

Resource mobilisation, partnerships, proposal coordination, and donor relations.

Finance & Administration

Financial management, administration, human resources, IT, and operational systems — led by the Finance & Administration Director.

Programs Manager

Programme design, implementation, technical quality, and learning — including supporting Program Officers and the Communications Consultants.

Communications Consultant

Leads storytelling, digital campaigns, and advocacy communications — producing content that amplifies youth voices, supports demand generation, and strengthens AFF’s visibility with communities, partners, and donors.

Monitoring & Evaluation

MEAL systems, research, and evidence generation — including M&E Officers and Researchers.

Community Ambassadors — "Ascenders"

Trusted young people recruited from within the communities they serve, supporting grassroots mobilisation and community engagement — alongside interns and volunteers.

Leadership profiles and photos are being added — meet the team on our socials in the meantime.

A Tiered Strategy: Lead, Build, Enable

Rather than spreading thin across many themes, we concentrate our strongest capabilities where we have proven credibility and community trust, while building carefully in emerging areas where the need is rising.

Core Priorities — Where We Lead

Our flagship pillars

🎗️

SRHR & HIV Prevention

Our flagship pillar: prevention literacy, demand generation, and access to youth-friendly, rights-based, choice-based services. Interventions span the full HIV prevention menu and the broader SRHR agenda:

  • Biomedical prevention literacy across oral PrEP, long-acting injectable Cabotegravir, the Dapivirine Vaginal Ring, Lenacapavir and other emerging options, PEP, and HIV testing services
  • Peer-led community demand generation, including digital SRHR & HIV prevention campaigns tailored to young people's realities
  • School and community-based life skills and comprehensive sexuality education
  • Community referral systems, service linkage, and capacity strengthening for healthcare workers and peer educators
📢

Community Advocacy & Systems Accountability

We implement, and we influence the systems around implementation — making systems more accountable and communities more able to hold them to account:

  • Community-led monitoring and accountability mechanisms
  • Youth advocacy and leadership development
  • Multi-stakeholder accountability dialogues, including on domestic resource mobilisation
  • Evidence-based policy advocacy on HIV prevention, SRHR, youth health, and climate-health intersections
  • Community feedback and client experience platforms
Strategic Growth Priorities — Where We Build

Phased, evidence-led expansion

🌍

Climate & Health Intersection

Climate change is reshaping community health, livelihoods, and vulnerability. Over this strategic period we are deliberately building capability at the climate-health intersection through institutional learning, a climate-health integration framework, evidence products and policy briefs, climate-sensitive community health approaches, and cross-sector partnerships — a phased, evidence-led approach rather than overstating current readiness.

🧠

Mental Health & Psychosocial Wellbeing

Mental health and psychosocial needs are rising among adolescents, young people, and caregivers — particularly within HIV, SRHR, and social vulnerability contexts. We are building foundational capacity through integration of psychosocial support into community health work, stigma reduction, youth resilience interventions, peer support, and partnerships with specialised institutions, ahead of larger-scale expansion.

Cross-Cutting Enablers

Research, Innovation & Community Engagement

These run underneath everything we do — strengthening evidence generation, adaptive implementation, policy influence, and community ownership rather than sitting as standalone programmes.

📱

Digital Innovation

Digital health information dissemination, mobile outreach and referral systems, community digital literacy, and technology-enabled peer support.

📊

MEAL & Adaptive Learning

Routine monitoring, operational research, rapid assessments, learning platforms, and data utilisation for adaptive, evidence-informed programming.

🗣️

Community Participation

Peer-led and community-led approaches, participatory planning and feedback, youth co-creation models, and community ownership mechanisms.

Where We Work

Lusaka & high-burden districts across Zambia

We are based in Lusaka and work across high-burden and underserved districts in Zambia — with rural and peri-urban communities affected by poverty, stigma, and gender inequality.
